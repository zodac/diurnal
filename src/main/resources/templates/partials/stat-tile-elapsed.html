{! The "how long ago was this?" sub-caption, shared by the three tiles whose sub is an elapsed span rather than a phrase of their own: first-performed, last-performed and last-day-with-multiples. All three resolve it identically from tile.subDaysAgo, so it is written once here instead of three times in partials/stat-tile-row. Params: tile — the StatTile being rendered compact — passed straight through to partials/stat-tile-dispatch label — the caption the caller already resolved (the user's rename, or the catalogue's word) subDaysAgo is -1 (never performed - or, for last-day-with-multiples, never performed more than once in one day - tile.value is "" in this case, so msg:neverLoggedIn supplies the word and the sub-caption stays the em dash placeholder, not a word), 0 (today), 1 (yesterday), or >= 2 (durationYears/Months/Days carry the elapsed span's breakdown, worded via msg:duration then wrapped by msg:statSubAgo). !} {#if tile.subDaysAgo == -1} {#include partials/stat-tile-dispatch compact=compact label=label value=msg:neverLoggedIn sub="—" subNum=tile.subNum valueClass=tile.valueClass date=tile.date /} {#else if tile.subDaysAgo == 0} {#let sub=msg:today}{#include partials/stat-tile-dispatch compact=compact label=label value=tile.value sub=sub subNum=tile.subNum valueClass=tile.valueClass date=tile.date /}{/let} {#else if tile.subDaysAgo == 1} {#let sub=msg:statSubYesterday}{#include partials/stat-tile-dispatch compact=compact label=label value=tile.value sub=sub subNum=tile.subNum valueClass=tile.valueClass date=tile.date /}{/let} {#else} {#let sub=msg:statSubAgo(msg:duration(tile.durationYears, tile.durationMonths, tile.durationDays))}{#include partials/stat-tile-dispatch compact=compact label=label value=tile.value sub=sub subNum=tile.subNum valueClass=tile.valueClass date=tile.date /}{/let} {/if}
