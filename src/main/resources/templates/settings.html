Two-column settings layout (flex, not grid). On desktop the left column stacks Account (top) then Preferences (below); the right column stacks Appearance then Statistics. Flex columns (rather than a grid) let each column pack its cards flush from the top independently — a grid would stretch the short left cards to the tall Appearance card's row height, leaving a gap under Account. Which column a card belongs in follows one rule: it goes wherever the first free vertical slot is. Preferences runs lower than Appearance, so Statistics (added after both) lands under Appearance on the right rather than under Preferences on the left. On MOBILE the order must be Account → Appearance → Preferences → Statistics. Because the cards are grouped into the two column wrappers (needed for the desktop columns), BOTH wrappers are `display:contents` below `lg` (`contents lg:flex…`): their boxes disappear on mobile so all four cards become direct flex items of THIS container, and `order-last` on Preferences and Statistics drops them (in source order) past Appearance. At `lg` the wrappers are real flex columns again and the order resets, so the desktop layout is unchanged. Each preference control saves ITSELF via its own PATCH endpoint (see the hx-patch on the radiogroups below and on the two selects in partials/select-field): updating one setting no longer touches the others, so there is no wrapper form and no shared/scoped trigger. This wrapper is just the layout container (and the delegation point for the "Saved" indicator in the script below). !}
